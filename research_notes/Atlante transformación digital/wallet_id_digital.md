## Key Question 1 — Which digital ID / membership / wallet programs generated measurable revenue, and how (numbers)?

### Takeaway
The programs with hard, recent revenue numbers are the Brazilian sócio-torcedor schemes (Flamengo R$88.1M in 2025 from 118k members; Palmeiras ~R$61M in Jan–Oct 2025 from ~168k members) and the European mass-membership clubs (Bayern 432,500 members, Barça €219/adult digital-only card). In Liga MX, paid digital memberships are small-ticket (América MXN 499/semester, Tigres "Socio Tigre Oro" MXN 500/season) and no club has published member counts or revenue; fan tokens and NFTs delivered one-off income in 2021–22 but have collapsed as a recurring revenue line.

### Inferences
- The revenue engine in every case with numbers is recurring paid membership tied to ticket priority (Brazil, Bayern, Barça); "digital ID" and "wallet" are the delivery vehicle, not the revenue source. For Atlante, the wallet/ID should be sized as the container for a paid tier (abono-lite / presale-priority) rather than as a fintech product.
- Brazil's fastest 2025 growth (Atlético-MG +36%, Corinthians +40%) came from free/cheap tiers with ticket priority, i.e. a funnel model: free ID → cheap tier → abono. Tigres' April 2026 design (free tier + MXN 500 Oro) copies this.
- Liga MX paid-membership price points cluster at MXN 499–500 per semester/season, which sets a market anchor for an "Atlante VIP" tier.
- Fan tokens/NFTs should be treated as optional, non-core, and reputationally risky; the recurring value they provide is voting/perks, which a club can deliver off-chain inside its own app.

## Key Question 2 — How does Liga MX Fan ID work today, and could Atlante's ID integrate with or extend it?

### Takeaway
Fan ID is a league-wide, Incode-built biometric identity (INE scan + selfie/liveness → personal QR) that is registered once at fanliga.mx / the Incode ID Wallet app and is valid at all 18 Liga MX stadiums; the FMF reported 3.59M registrations at the start of Clausura 2025 (Incode later cited 4.7M verified records). Enforcement has eroded (surveys report roughly half of fans are not asked for it), clubs resisted paying for gate infrastructure, and the data-consent model was criticised by INAI — so the ID exists as a data asset but not as a reliable access-control layer.

### Cited Findings
- Origin: Liga MX and FMF launched the Fan ID registration platform in November 2022, developed by Incode Technologies, after the Querétaro violence of March 2022; Incode said it worked with INAI on personal-data protection — [Grada, 18 Nov 2022](https://grada.mx/articles/futbol/2022/18/11/403/lanzan-liga-mx-y-fmf-plataforma-de-registro-fan-id); [PR Newswire / Incode, Nov 2022](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/liga-mx-adopts-incode-fan-id-across-stadiums-in-mexico-301683564.html); [Incode press](https://www.incode.com/press/liga-mx-adopts-incode-fan-id-across-stadiums-in-mexico); [Comunicae](https://comunicae.com.mx/notas-de-prensa/liga-bbva-mx-y-fmf-lanzan-fan-id-desarrollado)
- Mechanics: three steps — digital registration, biometric validation, unique QR; requires mobile number, email and a valid official ID (INE, passport or driving licence); registration is done once and applies at all 18 clubs' stadiums — [Récord, Oct 2025](https://www.record.com.mx/historia/liga-mx-como-hacer-el-registro-del-fan-id-para-entrar-a-los-estadios-en-2025102200295556017); [TUDN](https://tudn.com/amp/futbol/liga-mx/liga-mx-inicia-el-programa-fan-id-aficionados-ya-se-pueden-registrar-plataforma); [Stadibox guide](https://stadibox.com/blog/guias-y-consejos/fan-id-liga-mx-como-obtenerlo)
- Registration runs on fanliga.mx and the "Incode ID Wallet" app: phone number, photos of INE front/back, facial capture; output is a personal, non-transferable QR presented at the stadium — [Rebaño Pasión, Dec 2022](https://chivaspasion.bolavip.com/ligamx/Como-sacar-el-FAN-ID-para-ver-a-Chivas-en-el-Torneo-Clausura-2023--20221221-0008.html); [Mediotiempo explainer](https://www.mediotiempo.com/futbol/liga-mx/implementan-fan-id-en-la-liga-mx); [Digital Trends ES](https://es.digitaltrends.com/deportes/fan-id-liga-mexicana/)
- Roll-out was club-by-club: e.g. mandatory for Chivas home games from matchday 10 of Clausura 2023 — [Rebaño Pasión, Feb 2023](https://chivaspasion.bolavip.com/ligamx/Fan-ID-para-ver-a-Chivas-sera-obligatorio-en-la-Fecha-10-ante-Santos-PROBLEMAS--20230228-0004.html); Atlético de San Luis and Estadio Banorte (Azteca) maintain their own Fan ID pages — [Atlético de San Luis](https://www.atleticodesanluis.mx/noticias/357/Una-nueva-manera-de-disfrutar-nuestro-Futbol); [Estadio Banorte Fan ID](https://www.estadiobanorte.com.mx/en/fan-id)
- Adoption: FMF reported 3,585,258 registered fans at the start of Clausura 2025; Incode later published 4.7 million verified records — [ESPN Deportes](https://espndeportes.espn.com/futbol/mexico/nota/_/id/14878098/liga-mx-fan-id-aficionados-estadios-german-elvira-bloqueados); [Récord](https://www.record.com.mx/historia/liga-mx-como-hacer-el-registro-del-fan-id-para-entrar-a-los-estadios-en-2025102200295556017)
- Enforcement failure: authorities admit a 100% check is no longer done; in matchday 10 of Clausura 2025 ESPN reporters entered six high-attendance matches with only a ticket — [ESPN Deportes](https://espndeportes.espn.com/futbol/mexico/nota/_/id/14878098/liga-mx-fan-id-aficionados-estadios-german-elvira-bloqueados); a ClaroSports survey found 53% of fans were not asked for Fan ID at the gate and violence continued — [ClaroSports](https://www.clarosports.com/futbol/liga-mx/la-violencia-en-los-estadios-de-mexico-continua-tras-la-implementacion-del-fan-id-al-53-no-se-lo-piden-en-la-entrada/)
- Funding: Proceso (17 Jan 2025) describes Fan ID as "a promise that failed for lack of money": clubs were unwilling to assume the cost, so Fan ID scanning is slower than ticket scanning, creating queues, frustration and a violence risk; the measure "became diluted" — [Proceso](https://www.proceso.com.mx/deportes/2025/1/17/el-fan-id-una-promesa-que-fracaso-por-falta-de-dinero-342328.html)

- Club integration precedent: Club América's Membresía Azulcrema bundles Fan ID into the membership app flow — [Desde la Cuna](https://www.desdelacuna.net/club-america/donde-comprar-membresia-azulcrema-costo-beneficios-boletos-tienda/)
- Incode (the Fan ID vendor) acquired MetaMap in 2025, consolidating Mexican KYC vendors — [PR Newswire MX](https://prnewswire.com/mx/comunicados-de-prensa/incode-adquiere-metamap-para-liderar-el-futuro-de-la-verificacion-de-identidad-302168996.html)

### Inferences
- Fan ID is a Liga MX (first division) obligation; Atlante plays in Liga de Expansión MX, so no source found suggests Fan ID is mandatory at Atlante's home matches (see Gaps). Atlante can therefore design its own ID without a league dependency, but should keep the schema compatible (INE-verified identity + selfie + unique QR) so that a future promotion or league mandate would not require re-onboarding.
- The cheapest compatibility path is to use Incode as the KYC engine (same vendor as Fan ID), or at minimum store the same fields Fan ID collects; whether Liga MX/Incode expose an API for clubs to verify or link an existing Fan ID is unknown (Gap).
- Fan ID's failure modes are instructive: gate hardware/cost pushed onto clubs, slower scans than tickets, and weak consent language. An Atlante ID should (a) be embedded in the ticket QR rather than a second scan, (b) have a clear ARCO/consent flow under Mexico's data law, and (c) be voluntary-with-benefits rather than mandatory.

### Gaps
- No source found on whether Liga de Expansión MX clubs (incl. Atlante) are covered by, or excluded from, the Fan ID mandate.
- No public documentation of a Fan ID API for clubs or third-party ticketers; no information on whether ticketers (Ticketmaster México, Boletomóvil, Superboletos) validate Fan ID at purchase vs at the gate.
- No figure for what Fan ID costs the league or per-club gate hardware; Proceso's article (blocked) likely contains it.
- Actual 2026 enforcement status per stadium not verifiable (Mediotiempo/Crónica pages blocked).

## Key Question 3 — Recommended wallet architecture in Mexico (partner IFPE vs own stored value) and applicable compliance

### Takeaway
Holding user balances in Mexico requires an IFPE licence under the Ley Fintech (CNBV authorisation with SHCP and Banxico opinion, minimum capital, segregated funds, cybersecurity and AML controls) — a multi-year, capital-intensive path that most IFPEs are still losing money on. A club should not hold stored value itself; it should (a) run a closed-loop points/benefits ledger inside its app, (b) take payments via a licensed gateway (Openpay/Conekta/Stripe/Mercado Pago/Clip), (c) offer Apple/Google Pay and cards for cashless in-stadium spend, and (d) if a true balance is wanted, white-label or co-brand with an authorised IFPE (Spin by OXXO, Mercado Pago) or a bank.

### Cited Findings
- IFPE (Institución de Fondos de Pago Electrónico) is one of the two ITF types the CNBV can authorise to legally receive public funds — to issue, administer, redeem and transmit electronic payment funds — [Creditolab, 2026](https://creditolab.com/mx/noticias/fintech-mexicana-licencia-cnbv-2026); statute text: [Ley para Regular las Instituciones de Tecnología Financiera (Diputados PDF)](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LRITF.pdf); overview [Legal Paradox Ley Fintech 2026](https://www.legalparadox.com/es/ley-fintech)
- Authorisation requires CNBV approval with favourable opinion of SHCP and Banxico; applicants must show minimum capital, certified cybersecurity, AML policies aligned with CNBV/UIF, risk controls and corporate governance — [Nuvei, CNBV payment licence](https://www.nuvei.com/posts/how-to-obtain-a-cnbv-payment-license-in-mexico-for-fintech-and-digital-payment-operations); [Creditolab regulation guide](https://creditolab.com/mx/blog/regulacion-fintech-mexico-cnbv-2026)

### Inferences
- Architecture recommendation (derived): Layer 1 = identity (INE OCR + selfie liveness via Incode/Veridas/Nubarium, storing the same fields as Fan ID). Layer 2 = closed-loop loyalty ledger (points, vouchers, benefits) owned by the club — no fiat balance, so no IFPE licence. Layer 3 = payments via a licensed gateway/acquirer (Openpay/Conekta/Stripe/Clip) with Apple Pay/Google Pay and MSI for abonos. Layer 4 (optional, phase 2) = a co-branded account/card issued by an IFPE or bank partner (Spin, Mercado Pago, Nu, Banorte-style co-brand as Tigres/América/Chivas do with Banamex/Afirme), where the partner holds the funds and the KYC.
- Whether a club-issued closed-loop credit (e.g., "Atlante Coins" prepaid for stadium F&B) falls outside IFPE regulation depends on the statutory definition of fondos de pago electrónico and its exclusions; this was not verifiable in this session (statute PDF blocked) — needs legal opinion before launching any prepaid balance, even closed-loop.
- CoDi/DiMo are not a meaningful consumer rail for a fan app in 2026; SPEI and cards (plus OXXO cash-in via gateway) are.

## Key Question 4 — Vendors/platforms that could deliver an iOS+Android wallet/ID app in 6–9 months, and cost ranges

### Takeaway
Two viable routes: (1) a white-label sports platform (Fortress GB for ticketing/access/membership/loyalty/contactless; Tappit for in-app white-label payments or an app-less browser wallet; Fanisko "super app"; KORE and StellarAlgo for the data/CRM layer) or (2) a custom cross-platform build (Flutter or React Native) integrating a KYC SDK and a payment gateway. ## Key Question 5 — Which gamification / loyalty mechanics have worked best in football? (plus KPIs)

### Takeaway
The mechanics with documented results are ticket-linked: priority/presale access and cheap or free entry tiers (Atlético-MG +36%, Corinthians +40%, Flamengo +64% in 2025; Bayern Mastercard's 500 reserved tickets per match), plus tangible experiences (training visits, pitch-side access, meet & greets — the core of Tigres' and América's offers). Points-for-actions is now standard (Socio Tigre) but no club publishes engagement or ARPU results; crypto/NFT gamification proved confusing and speculative.

### Cited Findings
- Atlético-MG's record 144,431 members was "driven by the creation of popular and free categories with ticket-purchase priority" — [Fut1](https://www.fut1.com.br/2026/01/ranking-de-socios-torcedores-no-brasil.html)
- Flamengo's member base rose 64% (72k → 118k) in 2025, with R$88.1M program revenue — [Lance](https://www.lance.com.br/flamengo/flamengo-fecha-2025-com-r-21-bilhoes-de-receita-e-mais-de-118-mil-socios-torcedores.html)
- Bayern's FCB Mastercard reserves 500 tickets per regular home game for cardholders plus 5% store bonus — [Kreditkarten360](https://kreditkarten360.com/karte/fcb-mastercard/)
- Tigres Socio Tigre: earn points for actions across the club ecosystem, redeem for rewards/experiences; free tier = pitch-side at warm-ups, training visits, stadium tour, signed merch; Oro (MXN 500) = meet & greets, press-conference access, VIP tickets, team photo, early ticket sales, free shipping — [Récord](https://www.record.com.mx/historia/tigres-lanza-socio-tigre-asi-funciona-su-nuevo-programa-con-recompensas-y-beneficios-exclusivos-2026042103442479438); [ABC Noticias](https://abcnoticias.mx/deportes/tigres/2026/4/20/tigres-lanza-socio-tigre-su-programa-de-lealtad-para-aficionados-279403.html)
- Club América Membresía Azulcrema: presale for home games and Liguilla is the headline benefit, plus training visits, discounts and exclusive content — [Club América on X, May 2025](https://x.com/ClubAmerica/status/1924506223270863348); [Desde la Cuna](https://www.desdelacuna.net/club-america/donde-comprar-membresia-azulcrema-costo-beneficios-boletos-tienda/)

- Barça made the member card digital-only and uses the Members App for museum access and push notifications — [FC Barcelona](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/3819885/digital-membership-card-2025)
- Boca Socios app shows members their attendance record and dues status — [Marca en Zona](https://marcaenzona.com/news/post/boca-juniors-presento-su-nueva-app-boca-socios)
- Negative lessons: NBA Top Shot's gamification was called "too confusing" and a contributor to user decline; the collectibles market has been on a three-year cold streak — [The Ringer](https://www.theringer.com/2025/02/26/features/nba-top-shot-crypto-moments-bust-nfl-all-day-dapper-labs); [Sporting Crypto](https://newsletter.sportingcrypto.com/p/the-state-of-nba-top-shot-2025); fan tokens are event-driven and volatile (CHZ −47% in 30 days, June 2026) — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/football-transfer-market-crypto-fan-tokens/)

### Inferences
- The mechanic hierarchy suggested by the evidence: (1) ticket priority/presale and price advantages; (2) scarce physical experiences (training, pitch-side, meet & greet); (3) merchandise discounts and points; (4) content/badges/attendance streaks (Boca's attendance record is a cheap "streak" mechanic); (5) collectibles/tokens last, if at all.
- Suggested KPI set for Atlante (derived from what peers report): registered IDs; paid-tier members and conversion rate free→paid; renewal rate per semester; program revenue and ARPU (peers: Flamengo ≈ R$88.1M / 118k ≈ R$750/member/yr; Tigres Oro MXN 500/season; América MXN 499/semester); attendance per member and presale sell-through; in-stadium cashless spend per head; app MAU/DAU and wallet-pass installs.

- Juventus, PSG, Real Madrid Madridista-card and Barça "Culers" free-tier mechanics and numbers: not retrieved.
